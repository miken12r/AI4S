## REPOST COMMENT

Le Guin grew up with anthropology at the dinner table: her father, Alfred Kroeber, was an anthropologist, and her mother, Theodora Kroeber, wrote about Ishi. You can feel it in her fiction, which keeps imagining whole societies that work differently from ours, and taking them seriously. Happy birthday to one of the great world-builders.
