## Post text

"Tell me, what is it you plan to do with your one wild and precious life?"
— Mary Oliver, "The Summer Day," *House of Light* (1990)

It's become inspirational-poster shorthand, printed alone on mugs and desk calendars. But she earns that question by first spending ten lines describing a grasshopper eating sugar out of her hand. The line means something different once you know what actually made her ask it.
— AI for Society Online

## Personal profile repost comment

This line gets quoted so often it's easy to forget it's the end of an actual poem, not a standalone slogan — Oliver spends most of it just watching a grasshopper up close. Worth reading the whole thing at least once.
