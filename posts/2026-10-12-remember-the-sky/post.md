## Post text

"Remember the sky that you were born under,
know each of the star's stories.
Remember the moon, know who she is."
— Joy Harjo, "Remember" (1983)

Today the calendar says Columbus Day in some places, Indigenous Peoples' Day in others, and both in a few.
Harjo's poem doesn't argue about which. It asks you to remember something older than either.

## Personal profile repost comment

Joy Harjo taught herself the saxophone when she was nearly 40, and she still performs her poems with a band. In 2019 she became the first Native American U.S. Poet Laureate, and was asked to serve three terms. I like that "Remember" reads almost like a song — maybe it always was one.
