## Post text

"For most of history, Anonymous was a woman."
— Virginia Woolf, *A Room of One's Own* (1929)

Every unsigned poem in an old anthology needed a reason someone left their name off it. She's just asking who that reason usually was.

## Personal profile repost comment

The actual line is more careful than the version everyone shares: "I would venture to guess that Anon, who wrote so many poems without signing them, was often a woman." She's guessing, not declaring — which somehow makes it land harder, not softer.
