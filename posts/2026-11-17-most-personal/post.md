## POST TEXT

"The most personal is the most creative."
— Martin Scorsese, as quoted by Bong Joon Ho

In 2020, Bong Joon Ho won Best Director for Parasite. Martin Scorsese was one of the other nominees, sitting in the audience.

Bong told the room that as a film student he had carved that saying "deep into my heart," and then, in English: "That quote was from our great Martin Scorsese." He said he'd studied Scorsese's films in school and never thought he would win.

Then he offered to split the trophy with a Texas chainsaw and share it with the other four nominees.

Scorsese was born on this day in 1942. Some of the best tributes come from students who outgrew the classroom and still remembered where they learned.

## REPOST COMMENT

Bong gave most of the speech in Korean, with his interpreter Sharon Choi translating onstage, but he said one line himself in English: "That quote was from our great Martin Scorsese." Parasite had already won Original Screenplay and International Feature that night, and later in the evening it won Best Picture.
