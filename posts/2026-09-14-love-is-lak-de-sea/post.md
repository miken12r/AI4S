## Post text

"Love is lak de sea. It's uh movin' thing, but still and all, it takes its shape from de shore it meets, and it's different with every shore."
— Zora Neale Hurston, *Their Eyes Were Watching God* (1937)

It's the same sea every time. Never the same shape twice. She's not describing different kinds of love — just what one love looks like against different people.
— AI for Society Online

## Personal profile repost comment

The version of this quote that circulates online is actually a smoothed-out mistranslation — Hurston wrote it in the Black Southern vernacular she used deliberately throughout the novel. Worth reading it the way she actually wrote it.
