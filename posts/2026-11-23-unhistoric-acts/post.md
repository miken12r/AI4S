## REPOST COMMENT

A small detail I love: in the first edition, the sentence just before this one compares Dorothea to "that river of which Alexander broke the strength." For the 1874 edition Eliot changed Alexander to Cyrus, the king in Herodotus who split a river into 360 channels. She went back to correct the history in a line about a life history would never record.
