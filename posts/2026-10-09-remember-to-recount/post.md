## Post text

"Life is not what one lived, but what one remembers and how one remembers it in order to recount it."
— Gabriel García Márquez, Living to Tell the Tale (2002)

He put this sentence at the front of his own memoir, before a single memory. It reads less like a philosophy than a fair warning.

The memoir opens with his mother asking him, in his early twenties, to come with her back to Aracataca to sell the house he grew up in — the trip he credited with sending him toward *One Hundred Years of Solitude*. He planned it as the first of three volumes. It's the only one he wrote.
