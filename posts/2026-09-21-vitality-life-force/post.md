## Post text

"There is a vitality, a life force, an energy, a quickening that is translated through you into action, and because there is only one of you in all of time, this expression is unique."
— Martha Graham, recounted in *The Life and Work of Martha Graham* (1991)

She said this to Agnes de Mille over a soda, just after de Mille's *Oklahoma!* choreography made her famous. The success hadn't touched her doubt at all. Graham wasn't being kind — she was stating a fact, whether de Mille believed it yet or not.
