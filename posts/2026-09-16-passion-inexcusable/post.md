## Post text

"To play a wrong note is insignificant; to play without passion is inexcusable."
— Attributed to Ludwig van Beethoven

No one can actually find him writing this down. It's probably not really his. Two centuries of people repeating it anyway is its own kind of passion over precision.

## Personal profile repost comment

If you want something he definitely wrote, look up his 1801 letter to Wegeler instead: "I will seize Fate by the throat." But somehow this one's the line that outlived him.

## Note on attribution

This quote is widely circulated but disputed/unverified — it doesn't appear in any known Beethoven writing. Likely a modern paraphrase of secondhand accounts from his student Ferdinand Ries. Used here as "Attributed to" rather than a direct quote, per the project's accuracy standard.
