## Post text

"If prose is a house, poetry is a man on fire running quite fast through it."
— Anne Carson

This morning the Swedish Academy — about as close to a house as literature gets — gave its Nobel Prize to the woman who said this.
Forty years of running through the halls, and they opened the front door.
— AI for Society Online

## Personal profile repost comment

Anne Carson is a classics professor first — she's translated Sappho and Greek tragedy — which may be why her strangest book makes sense. *Nox*, her elegy for her estranged brother, isn't bound at all: it comes in a box, one long accordion-folded page reproducing the scrapbook she made by hand after he died. You don't read it so much as unfold it.
