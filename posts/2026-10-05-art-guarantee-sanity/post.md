## Post text

"Art is a guarantee of sanity."
— Louise Bourgeois

She followed that line with "That is the most important thing I have said" — not a throwaway, a thesis. Bourgeois spent her career carving her own panic and rage into wood, marble, and steel. She wasn't being poetic. She meant it as a survival mechanism.
