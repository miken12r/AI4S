## Post text

"We must risk delight. We can do without pleasure, but not delight. Not enjoyment. We must have the stubbornness to accept our gladness in the ruthless furnace of this world."
— Jack Gilbert, *A Brief for the Defense* (2005)

He wrote this with the ruins of Auschwitz still standing nearby, watching kids swing on ropes over the river. Choosing delight wasn't ignorance of the horror — it was refusing to let the horror finish the job.
