## Post text

"The mother art is architecture. Without an architecture of our own we have no soul of our own civilization."
— Frank Lloyd Wright

He's not saying architecture is the prettiest art. He's saying it's the one you can't escape from — you live inside it, every day, whether or not you ever notice it was designed at all.
— AI for Society Online

## Personal profile repost comment

Wright built Fallingwater — a house cantilevered directly over a waterfall — because he thought people should live inside nature's structure, not next to it. "Mother art" wasn't a slogan for him. It was a design brief.
