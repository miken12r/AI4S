## Source note

Quote is from Rainer Maria Rilke's *Letters to a Young Poet*, Letter IV
(16 July 1903). Well-documented, widely translated correspondence —
used as a direct quote.

Bio detail verified: Rilke's correspondent was Franz Xaver Kappus, a
19-year-old officer cadet at the Theresian Military Academy. Their
correspondence ran 1902–1908; Kappus published the letters in 1929,
three years after Rilke's death in 1926 (per Wikipedia / standard
publication history).

## Post text

"Be patient toward all that is unsolved in your heart, and try to love the questions themselves."
— Rainer Maria Rilke, Letters to a Young Poet (1903)

He wrote this to a 19-year-old military cadet, Franz Xaver Kappus, who'd sent him anxious poems and an even more anxious question: should he leave the army for poetry? Rilke never actually answered that. He told him to stop needing an answer.
