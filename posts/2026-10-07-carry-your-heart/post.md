## Post text

"i carry your heart with me(i carry it in my heart)"
— e.e. cummings

He wrote it with no punctuation and no capital letters, the words fused shut — he wanted you to read it without a single breath between "carry" and "heart." The form wasn't a gimmick. It was the whole point.
