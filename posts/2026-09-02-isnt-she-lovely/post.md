## Post text

"Isn't she lovely, isn't she wonderful, isn't she precious, less than one minute old"
— Stevie Wonder, "Isn't She Lovely," *Songs in the Key of Life* (1976)

No metaphor, no clever comparison — just three plain words, asked as questions, like he's still catching up to what just happened. Sometimes astonishment doesn't need better language. It just needs repeating.
