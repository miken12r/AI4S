## Post text

"A second spring, when every leaf's a flower."
— Albert Camus, spoken by the character Jan in *The Misunderstanding* (1944)

Out of context, it sounds like a celebration of fall. In the play, Jan says it to comfort Martha about a bleak, joyless place — a hopeful line spoken into a story that doesn't believe it. The equinox holds both at once: the light is balanced, but it's still tipping toward the dark half of the year.
— AI for Society Online

## Personal profile repost comment

Today's the equinox, so this felt fitting. But it's also classic Camus: the quote everyone shares as "Camus on the beauty of autumn" is actually a character consoling someone in one of his bleakest plays—right before the story turns to tragedy. So, no matter how you feel about the changing of the seasons, this one works for you!
