## Source note

Akira Kurosawa, quoted in Donald Richie's *The Films of Akira Kurosawa*
(1965): "I think that to find what is real one must look very closely at
one's world.... To be an artist means never to avert one's eyes."
(per Encyclopedia.com's Kurosawa entry, citing Richie).

**Not** from Kurosawa's own *Something Like an Autobiography* (1981/82),
even though quote sites commonly say so — card and post attribute it to
Kurosawa only, without a book title.

Commentary fact (Great Kantō earthquake, 1923): his older brother Heigo
took 13-year-old Akira through the ruins of Tokyo and wouldn't let him
look away from the dead; Kurosawa tells this in *Something Like an
Autobiography*, and drew the lesson that looking at a frightening thing
head-on defeats its power to frighten. (Corroborated via Wikipedia and
akirakurosawa.info.)

Selection: first filmmaker in the series; first Asian voice since Ocean
